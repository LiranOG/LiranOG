### A Note on My Development Ecosystem:
I am a solo architect and developer behind all the projects, theories, and repositories presented here. Because my work spans across highly complex and diverse fields—from theoretical physics to AI-driven Systems. most of the repositories here are active works-in-progress.  
They are currently in various active stages of development, ranging from initial architectural design to advanced implementation.

While not everything is perfectly polished yet, I am continuously iterating and deeply committed to bringing every single project to the highest standard of completion.


> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Personal Hiatus — May to August 2026
>
> I am stepping back from active development on all of my projects
> for a few months to attend to matters in my personal life. To be
> explicit: **nothing has been abandoned.** Every repository remains
> public, every published artifact is stable in its current state,
> and I fully intend to return to each project.
>
> **Where each project stands at the start of the hiatus:**
>
> - **[GRANITE-NR](https://github.com/LiranOG/Granite-NR)** —
>   Numerical relativity and GRMHD engine in C++17. **Current
>   release `v0.6.8`** (*Architecture & Stability*, 9 May 2026).
>   105 / 107 GoogleTest cases passing across 20 suites;
>   `single_puncture` and `B2_eq` benchmarks validated stable
>   through t = 500 M. The engine is buildable, runnable, and
>   citable
>   (DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.19502265](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19502265)).
>   The `v0.7.0 → v1.0.0` roadmap resumes after the hiatus.
>
> - **[VORTEX](https://github.com/LiranOG/VORTEX)** —
>   *VORTEX ETERNITY.* Interactive relativistic N-body simulator
>   running entirely in the browser. 2.5PN dynamics, full GW strain,
>   Hermite-4 integrator with Kahan compensated summation,
>   18 astrophysical scenarios (GW150914, GW170817, EMRI, ZKL, Sgr A*,
>   N-body cascade, Solar System, …). **The live demo at
>   [liranog.github.io/VORTEX](https://liranog.github.io/VORTEX/)
>   remains fully operational throughout the hiatus** — no install,
>   no dependencies, open in any modern browser. The single documented
>   open issue (the missing 2PN conservative correction term) and any
>   further feature work pause until I return.
>
> - **[LOITER-SIM](https://github.com/LiranOG/LOITER-SIM)** —
>   Deterministic multi-agent simulation engine for autonomous UAV
>   swarms. **Pre-alpha, design phase — no executable code is published
>   yet.** The repository currently contains the architecture
>   specification, physics and sensor specifications, governance
>   structure, and the milestone roadmap targeting **v1.0 in Q3 2028**
>   with implementation start scheduled for **Q1 2027**. All
>   specification documents remain readable and open to expert critique
>   during the hiatus. Per the project's own FAQ, the binding test is
>   whether committed implementation code appears by **Q2 2027**; the
>   August 2026 return leaves that window fully intact.
